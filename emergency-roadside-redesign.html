Roadside decision guide

What should I do right now?

Choose what happened. We'll tell you the safe first steps and which kind of help to look for.

Car won't move

After an accident, a breakdown or a stuck vehicle, safety comes before anything else.

  • Turn on hazard lights and move to the shoulder if you safely can.
  • Stay away from moving traffic — wait behind a barrier if possible.
  • Note your exact location, mile marker or nearest exit.
  • Ask the tow company for a total price before they dispatch.

  • Low or AWD car → Flatbed tow
  • Stuck in mud / ditch → Winch-out
  • RV or truck → Heavy-duty tow

Car won't start

Clicking, dim lights or nothing at all usually points to the battery.

  • Check if dash lights come on when you turn the key.
  • Rapid clicking usually means a weak or flat battery.
  • If it starts after a jump but dies again, ask for a battery test.
  • EV owners: look for providers that list EV support.
Look for
Battery help

  • Old battery → Mobile replacement
  • Still dead → Mobile mechanic

Flat tire

Don't drive far on a flat — it can damage the wheel and make the car harder to control.

  • Pull over on flat, firm ground away from traffic.
  • Check you have a usable spare and a jack.
  • Slow leak? Mobile inflation may get you to a shop.
  • No spare or locking nut key? Ask for a tow instead.
Look for
Flat tire help
  • Have a spare → Tire change
  • Slow leak → Inflation
  • No spare → Tow to tire shop
Find tire help

Locked out

Keys inside the car, a snapped key or no key at all — each needs a different kind of help.

  • A child or pet locked inside? Call 911 immediately.
  • Have your ID and proof of ownership ready.
  • Lost key or keyless fob? Ask if they can program keys on site.
  • Confirm the call-out fee and total before they arrive.
Look for
Car lockout
  • Keys inside → Lockout service
  • Broken key → Key extraction
  • Lost key → Auto locksmith
Find lockout help

Out of fuel

Fuel delivery is usually quicker and cheaper than a tow to the nearest station.

  • Tell the provider whether you need gasoline or diesel.
  • Share your exact location and vehicle type.
  • Stay in a safe spot off the road while you wait.
  • EV out of charge? Search for EV help or a flatbed tow.
Look for
Fuel delivery
  • Gas or diesel → Fuel delivery
  • EV flat → EV help / flatbed

Noise or warning light

Not every warning light means a tow. A mobile mechanic can often diagnose it where you are.

  • Red light (oil, temperature, brakes)? Stop driving safely.
  • Amber light? Usually safe to reach a shop soon.
  • Note any smells, smoke or noises to describe them.
  • Need a longer repair? Browse local repair shops.

Mobile mechanic

  • Bigger job → Auto repair shop
